Prescribe the mechanics

Create a camera pair

Inverse warping moves a seeded speckle pattern. Blur, brightness, independent noise and 8-bit quantization form the measured images.

03

Measure independently

ZNCC locates subsets. Six-parameter affine optimization refines displacement. Invalid or weak matches are rejected.

04

Recover & validate

Local polynomial fits give strain. Plane-stress elasticity gives stress. Results are compared at the same reference centres.
